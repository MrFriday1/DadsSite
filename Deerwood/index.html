About Us

A Legacy of Quality
Since 1987

Founded in 1987, Deerwood Custom Millwork has grown from a local Brampton workshop into a premier destination for architectural woodwork in the GTA. Our management team brings over 75 years of combined millwork experience, offering industrial products, nationwide delivery, and the expertise to bring new concepts to light.
